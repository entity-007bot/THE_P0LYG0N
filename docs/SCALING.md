## From 10k to 10M: Edge-First Scaling

### Phase 1: Pilot (10k users, 1 LGA)
- 5 Android edge nodes (Termux)
- Local SQLite, sync nightly
- Squad test keys
- ₦40M – ₦70M budget 
- ) 1 LGA; Unit economics & basic platform integration.

### Phase 2: City (250k users, 6 LGAs)
- 20 edge nodes + 1 regional aggregator
- PostgreSQL for each region
- Squad live keys + webhook retry queue
- ₦150M – ₦300M budget 
- Lagos/Abuja; VSLA groups & scaling support systems.

### Phase 3: National (5M+ users, 36 states)
- Edge nodes: 1 per LGA (774 total)
- Central policy DB for fraud detection
- Squad enterprise tier (discounted tx fees)
- CDN for model updates (Xenova embeddings)
- ₦800M – ₦1.5B budget
- All 36 states; Full government & bank infrastructure.

### Cost per user at scale
- Edge node: $15/month (runs on old Android)
- Bandwidth: $0.01/user for sync
- Squad fees: 0.8% avg (negotiated)
- → Unit margin: $1.20/user/year

- 5M workers × avg 2.3 jobs/week × ₦1500/job
